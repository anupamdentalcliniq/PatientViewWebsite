About the clinic

Personalised, specialty dental care under one roof.

Founded by Dr. Aruna and Dr. Annamalai, Anupam Dental Clinic has spent over fourteen years providing personalised specialty dental care in Madipakkam. As a clinic run by a prosthodontist and an orthodontist, the team takes particular pride in smile design, smile makeovers and full mouth rehabilitation — alongside everyday family dentistry.

The approach is conservative first: save the natural tooth wherever possible, explain every option in plain language, and only proceed once a patient is comfortable with the plan.

Services at Anupam Dental Clinic

Root Canal Treatment

Orthodontics & Braces

Metal, ceramic and lingual braces plus clear aligners for all ages.

Dental Implants

Single-tooth to full-arch implants for a permanent, natural-feeling fix.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Complete restoration of function and appearance across all teeth.
